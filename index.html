How to launch
  1. Press W / GAS to start the engine.
  2. Hold S (brake), then hold W too — launch control holds the revs at your launch rpm (set it in the Car tab) and builds boost.
  3. Watch the tree: three ambers… then let go of the brake. Leave early and you get a red light.
  4. Stay on the gas. Auto shifts for you — or shift yourself with + / − (switches to manual; M toggles back).
  5. Brake with S (tap it to modulate without ABS), P pops the parachute if fitted. C changes camera, R resets to the start line, Enter starts/stops the engine.

Welcome to the engine lab

  • Pick parts on the left: layout, cylinders, bore × stroke, cams, turbos, fuel, tune, exhaust, hybrid.
  • Power and torque curves update live as you change things. Pin a baseline to compare.
  • Start the engine and rev it — the sound is synthesized from each cylinder's real firing order.
  • Run a dyno pull, take it to the drag strip, and try the challenges. Push too hard and it might blow.
  • Try Career mode (top right): start with a junker, win races, buy parts, climb to Legend.
